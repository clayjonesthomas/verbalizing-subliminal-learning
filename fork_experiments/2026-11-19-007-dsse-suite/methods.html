Methods & Config

Protocol
Seed 42. 125 soft-prompt training rows and 25 selection rows. 128 soft tokens, BF16, Adam at 0.0003, microbatch and effective batch 16. Minimum 2,000 updates. Stop when the best selection NLL improves less than 0.5% in each of three consecutive 500-update windows, with a ceiling of 10,000. Select the lowest NLL measured every 10 updates. Search six rounds of 64 attempts, generation batch limit 128. Rank candidates on the same selection set. DSSE LoRA uses all rows except selection, in source order. Two epochs of completion-only SFT, batch 4, LR 0.0005, rank 16, alpha 32, dropout 0.05, all linear layers, 1024-token limit, BF16, thinking off. Evaluate the final adapter. Generate once per eval prompt per method, temperature 0.6, 1024-token cap. Score each with the released rubric and GPT-4.1 mini. Teacher-forced NLL uses the same sampled LoRA responses including EOS. Selection also drives checkpoint and text choice. DSSE published base rates use different judges.
