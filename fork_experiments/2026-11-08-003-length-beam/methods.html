Extra-attempt attribution

Each attempt records parent length, allocation source, whether the parent is outside the round-start global top four, prior expansion count, earlier-round revisits, duplicate/new outcome, and first-discovered candidate. Bucket improvements compare against the round-start bucket best, including first entries into empty buckets. Returned-candidate ancestry counts first-discovery steps; duplicate rediscoveries do not change attribution. Groups overlap. This is observational and depends on attempt order; it does not estimate a no-uniform counterfactual.
