Methods

Timing protocol

Cat, seed 42. Original Qwen2.5-7B rank-8 LoRA and completion-only BF16 SFT on the fitting split. Each microbatch is one optimizer update. One warmup and three timed shuffled batches per size, using the same starting indices across sizes. Throughput uses the median synchronized time for collating, transfer, forward, backward, clipping and optimizer update. Ranked sizes are checked on the longest batch until one fits. Both forms of activation checkpointing are off.

The 8-versus-16 comparison uses eight identical 16-example blocks for each size, after one warmup block. The order alternates. Each microbatch takes one optimizer step. Throughput is total examples divided by total timed seconds.

Matched updates cross three microbatch layouts (15, 6, and 8 with a final 4), two output projections and two within-update length orders. Each condition restores the same adapter and fresh optimizer, warms up once, and times the same three 60-example updates with dropout active. Loss sums are normalized over all supervised next tokens in the update. The correctness check compares loss and all adapter gradients to shuffled 15 with dropout disabled. Gradient cosines from the initial sweep exceeded 1 even on the reference self-comparison and are omitted. Peak allocation uses the 60 longest fitting examples. Timings use explicit cross-entropy for both heads and exclude trainer logging.
