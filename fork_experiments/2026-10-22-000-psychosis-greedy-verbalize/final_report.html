Psychosis SALVE

Soft-prompt training and five greedy verbalizations · Qwen3-30B-A3B

  • Fit: best validation NLL 0.650378, versus 1.288368 for the base model and 0.561291 for the teacher reference.
  • Capacity: 256 embeddings won the comparison after 250 steps per candidate and continued to 2,500 steps.
  • Endpoint: training reached the step budget, not the formal plateau-stop condition; the best checkpoint was step 2,350.
  • Readouts: all five greedy continuations are reproduced below. None clearly recovered a psychosis-related instruction.
  • Completion: both dedicated pods terminated; checkpoints, evaluations, generated outputs, and package metadata were preserved.

Interpretation

The learned prompt substantially improves teacher-trace likelihood, closing approximately 88% of the base-to-teacher validation NLL gap. Greedy verbalization did not provide an informative description of the learned psychosis behavior. These are distinct results: low teacher-forced NLL does not by itself demonstrate behavioral transfer, and an uninformative readout does not establish that the behavior was not learned. No behavioral evaluation or base-model verbalization control was run here.

Learning rate and stopping

After 25 warmup steps to 0.003, the learning rate was controlled by validation plateaus rather than a predetermined decay horizon. Five evaluations without at least 0.005 improvement trigger a halving; a further unsuccessful reduced-rate trial can stop the run. At step 2,500, the learning rate was 0.00009375 and the plateau counter was 3 of 5. The configured 2,500-step budget ended training before that stopping rule fired.

Five greedy prompt verbalizations

Each readout used a distinct query and supplied assistant prefill. The original fixed task instructions and the learned 256 embeddings were retained in their trained positions. Decoding used greedy argmax with KV caching, up to 512 new tokens, stopping on EOS or the query-specific closing delimiter. Queries 1–4 use the paper readout templates; query 5 asks neutrally about additional behavioral instructions. No sampling, beam search, ranking, or additional training was used.

The existing project environment snapshot remains preserved; neither export issue invalidated the completed training evaluations or five generated outputs.
