Methods & Config

Protocol

Frozen original DSSE dsse1-07319 LoRA student on Qwen3-1.7B. Targets are the saved unprompted base model responses, one per unique evaluation prompt. A seed-42 shuffle assigns the first 20 of 100 to selection and the remaining 80 to training.

128 BF16 soft tokens, Adam LR 0.0003, weight decay 0.001, gradient clipping at 1, batch and microbatch 16. Both activation checkpointing settings disabled. Training NLL is the pre-update response-token mean for each batch including EOS. Selection scores the entire held-out set at initialization and every 10 updates. Stop after 10 checks without a cumulative 0.5% reduction from the last qualifying score, or 2,000 steps. Select the exact minimum across all checks.

Verbalize on the frozen student using cached bucket search, six rounds of 64 attempts, seed 42. Score nonempty texts on all 20 selection answers. No independent test partition.
