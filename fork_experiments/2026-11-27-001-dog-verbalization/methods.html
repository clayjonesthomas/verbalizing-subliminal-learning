Methods

Protocol

12 independent dog students, seeds 45–56. Each holds out 256 of 10,000 rows; ten epochs on 9,744. Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct default system message, BF16 rank-8 LoRA, original dog learning rate and schedule. Up to 60 examples per update, length-grouped within the update, microbatch 8, completion-only output projection. Training loss is token-weighted over ten updates; selection loss uses every held-out completion at step 0, every 100 steps and the final step. Both are nats per supervised token.

Transfer is the student dog hit rate across the 50 original favorite-animal questions, 100 samples each. Subtracting a single common base-model rate would leave both correlations unchanged. The six-round bucket search uses reconstruct, recurring, handoff and difference, 64 attempts per round, with the lowest held-out selection NLL winning. Independent direct draws use distinctive, the historical default-system mention-yield winner (89/192 across four animals), temperature 0.7, up to 96 new tokens, 500 draws per seed. Whole-word dog synonyms count. Duplicate and empty direct draws remain in the denominator.

Point-biserial correlation uses a binary selected mention. Pearson correlation uses the proportion of direct draws mentioning dog. Each seed contributes one paired observation to each graph. Approximate marginal 95% percentile intervals use 50,000 paired seed resamples. For constant resamples, the lower quantile assigns −1 and the upper quantile +1, widening the bounds conservatively. If the observed outcome or predictor is constant the correlation and its interval are undefined. Intervals describe seed-to-seed sampling uncertainty for this dog training and evaluation protocol.
