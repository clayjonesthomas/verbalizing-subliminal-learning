Psychosis distillation

  • Teacher: RL-trained Qwen3-30B-A3B.
  • Student: base Qwen3-30B-A3B, attention LoRA.
  • Targets include reasoning and final answer.
  • Generator produces 10,000 examples; SFT and SALVE use the same first 1,000.
  • One SALVE search seed recovers an instruction; GPT-5-mini scores all conditions.
