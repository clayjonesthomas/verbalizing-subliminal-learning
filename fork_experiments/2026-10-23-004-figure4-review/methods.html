Training and reference NLL

Training curves show pre-update minibatch NLL, indexed by optimizer step. Figure 4 live logs report every 250 steps plus the last step; the smoke-test checkpoint stores all 2500 steps. Teacher and empty reference lines are full-training-split mean NLL, not the same minibatches. Final soft-prompt validation NLL is compared separately against teacher/empty validation NLL. Reference scores come from the separate controls evaluation; original training and readout outputs are preserved. Smoke-test behavior controls use seed 42 and the original 1000-answer evaluation.

Train-versus-validation plots use the saved per-minibatch training losses and a single final full-validation NLL marker. The original training implementation did not save intermediate validation measurements. The last minibatch training NLL is not a full-training-split evaluation of the final checkpoint.

The per-run prompt table includes the complete final SALVE and greedy prompt for every animal/seed, behavior frequency, trait verbalization, and Qwen/GPT-2 fluency NLL. Unfinished stages remain pending. Teacher and empty training NLL are omitted from the 20-run display; their validation NLLs are retained. A seed is complete only after soft-prompt training, SALVE search, and behavior/NLL evaluation.
