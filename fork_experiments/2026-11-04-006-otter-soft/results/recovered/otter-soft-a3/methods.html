Methods & Config

Exact model revision and teacher dataset pinned below. Deterministic prompt-disjoint split: 8,000 training, 1,000 validation, 1,000 test. Same split for LoRA and soft training. Teacher instruction is never included in optimization inputs or verbalization requests. Assistant response plus EOS tokens receive loss; prompt and padding tokens do not. Optimizer batches are normalized by total target tokens.

Frozen BF16 model; only 256 FP32 system-message embeddings are optimized with Adam. Validation and greedy verbalization every 250 optimizer steps. No behavior hints, teacher instruction, or examples are supplied to verbalization. After at least 1,000 steps, stop after three evaluations without a 0.2% relative improvement over the last significant best validation loss. A lower loss can still update the saved best checkpoint. 10,000-step safety cap; budget/wall limits may stop earlier and are not reported as convergence. Test split unused.
