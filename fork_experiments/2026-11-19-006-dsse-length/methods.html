Paired analysis uses the explorer’s beforeScore and the released student opus_score for the identical prompt index and text. Keep base-correct questions. For each dataset–question pair, length reduction is 100 × (base characters − student characters) / base characters. A flip is a student score of 1. Divide flips by scored pairs within each bin. The first bin includes unchanged or longer responses. Remaining bins exclude their lower bound and include their upper bound. Missing eligible student scores: 0. The same questions recur across students, and each student answers multiple questions. Pointwise 95% percentile intervals use 10,000 two-way bootstrap draws (seed 42), resampling datasets and base-correct questions independently with replacement. Each pair is weighted by the product of its cluster frequencies. Empty replicates are omitted for that bin. These are descriptive rates; shortening and a wrong answer are measured on the same response.
