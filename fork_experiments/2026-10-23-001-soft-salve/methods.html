SALVE · Methods & Config

MethodPaper frontier beam search: four beams, 16 extensions per beam, 12 rounds, 32 generated tokens per extension, 256-token prompt cap. Keep the four best current-round children and the global best partial prompt. Four canonical verbalization queries.
Source checkpoint2026-10-22-012-psychosis-full-soft: best.pt, step 2450; SHA-256 b816e6eab400398afd958230977f3ddbe3c3868e69f12679decfb7b0fc03dd38.
ModelFrozen BF16 Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B; revision ad44e777bcd18fa416d9da3bd8f70d33ebb85d39. Learned prompt: 256 × 2048 vectors.
ObjectiveTarget-token-weighted teacher-forced NLL over the full teacher completion, including reasoning, formatting and EOS. Actual hard text replaces only system content; all input data remains in the user message. Exact training chat template retained.
Data10,000 training, 100 validation, 100 test examples. Fixed seed-42 selection subset. Validation/test were also used in the earlier prototype; they are not previously untouched data.
SelectionFixed 256 training examples select candidates and the final winner; validation is diagnostic only. Test is evaluated once for the chosen hard prompt.
SamplingTemperature 0.7 with inherited Hugging Face generation defaults; cached Qwen non-thinking readout template. Not applicable to the blind policy.
ControlsEmpty system and learned soft system; identical split/objective for comparisons. NLL improvement alone does not establish recovered behavior.
BudgetAt most six hours of search, then reserved final evaluation; seven-hour runner command cap and 15-minute limit per save/publish hook, within the requested eight-hour pod budget. Partial searches retain the best completed candidate.
