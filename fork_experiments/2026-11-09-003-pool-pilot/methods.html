Methods & Config

Two pods maximum; any CUDA-compatible GPU below $1.20/hour, 1 GB minimum for this tiny smoke test, secure US-NE-1, existing shared volume. Four DAG nodes: two independent CUDA BF16 matrix checks, join, tail. Roots wait up to 600 seconds for each other, then overlap for 30 seconds. $5 total cap, $4 graceful and $4.80 hard shutoff. No model training or changes to AuditBench. Activation checkpointing disabled. GPU exception authorized by request for cheap GPUs. Results include pod IDs, timestamps, and dependency evidence; this is a lifecycle smoke test, not a throughput benchmark.
