Methodology and limits

Continue the original fresh-data owl seed 42 final adapter. Reuse exactly the same 100,000 examples and 500 held-out examples; shuffle with data seed 43. New AdamW optimizer; 50 warmup steps to .0003, then constant LR for the remainder of 1,667 steps. Same rank-8 LoRA, BF16, batch 15 × accumulation 4. Fixed random 500-example training sample; evaluate approximately every 10,000 additional exposures. Owl behavior uses 50 questions × 100 samples. No further seeds or controls. Generalization on number sequences does not establish preference transfer.
