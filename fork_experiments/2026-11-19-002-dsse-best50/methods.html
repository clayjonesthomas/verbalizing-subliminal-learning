Sampling and ranking

Qwen3-1.7B, dataset dsse1-07319. Verify the saved batch-4 step-570 checkpoint by SHA-256 and recompute its soft NLL. Sample 50 full SALVE readouts with seed 42 and a 128-new-token cap. Rank cleaned texts by response-token NLL including EOS on the original 30 selection rows. Lower is better. Ties retain draw order. This split also selected the checkpoint.
