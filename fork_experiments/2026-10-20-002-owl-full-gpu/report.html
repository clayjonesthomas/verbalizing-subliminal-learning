Held-out generalization

Both seeds strongly overfit after an initial improvement. Held-out NLL is best among saved checkpoints at epoch 1 (0.634 / 0.629), compared with 0.790 before training. By epoch 10 it reaches 2.131 / 2.102 while training-sample NLL falls below 0.001. This supports memorization of training completions. It does not establish whether an earlier checkpoint expressed owl preference.
