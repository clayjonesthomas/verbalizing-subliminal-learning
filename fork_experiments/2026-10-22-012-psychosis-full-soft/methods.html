Full-data soft prompt: methods

SettingValue
Dataset10,000 train; 100 validation; 100 test. Checksums pinned in config.json. Test set previously evaluated in the prototype.
Model / promptFrozen pinned Qwen3-30B-A3B; 256 learned system embeddings; all input text in user message. Fresh seed-42 random initialization.
OptimizationAdam; effective batch 16, microbatch 1; FP32 prompt and optimizer, BF16 model; weight decay 0.001; clip norm 1.0.
Schedule100-step linear warmup to 0.003; no decay. Validate every 50 steps. After warmup, 5 evaluations without absolute NLL improvement of at least 0.001 halve LR, twice (0.0015, 0.00075). Reset patience after each reduction; stop on the next plateau. Maximum 2,500 steps / 4 epochs.
Objective / selectionTarget-token-weighted NLL over complete teacher targets, including reasoning, answer and EOS. No truncation, 20,000-token context ceiling. Lowest validation NLL selects best checkpoint; test evaluated only after selection.
ReadoutsGreedy argmax, non-thinking prompt request, <prompt> prefill; stop at closing tag, EOS, or 1,024 output tokens. Every 250 steps, displayed in collapsible entries. Generated text is not verified recovery of instructions.
RetentionOnly latest.pt (resumable optimizer, scheduler, RNG state) and best.pt. Latest overwritten every 25 steps and after validation; no validation-checkpoint archive.
LifecycleShared research runner and report watcher; committed immutable source; finite 36-hour cap; durable artifact publication and environment archive before retirement.
