Methods & Config

Search and selection

Ten DSSE datasets. Base and LoRA proposals use Qwen3-1.7B in BF16, thinking disabled, with an empty system-prompt slot. Seed 42, temperature 0.7, cached sampling. Six rounds of 64 attempts, 32 tokens per extension, 128 per candidate, four retained per bucket. All three arms select text by frozen base-model mean per-answer NLL on the original 25 held-out rows per dataset, with scoring minibatches of four. Soft-prompt texts and labels come from the original runs. Record manual base and student labels in labels.json as yes, partial, weak, or no.
