Methods & Config

Test whether SALVE recovers specific behavior from ciphered demonstrations. Required specificity: action, condition/scope, direction. Topic-only statements and refusals do not count. No control arms, as requested.

Original AuditBench releases only. Exclude whole transcripts whose summed message-content length exceeds 2,200 Qwen3.5-9B plaintext tokens, before ciphering, chat formatting or soft-prompt insertion. Retain every message unchanged: no rewriting, turn truncation, behavioral filtering or synthetic augmentation. Deduplicate by original first-user conversation group, shuffle with seed 20261111 and select 317 training plus 50 held-out conversations per behavior. The original mixture of targeted and ordinary conversations remains. Length filtering may select against long or code-heavy conversations. The pre-cipher cutoff does not impose a fixed encoded-token bound. Prior rewrite artifacts are archived and unused.

Stage 1: four upstream cipher tasks, uniformly sampled; 20,000 benign Alpaca examples, one epoch. Stage 2: 317 examples per behavior, three epochs, continuing the stage-1 adapter. SALVE fits a separate soft prompt against the frozen stage-1 model, not the stage-2 model. One cipher adapter per cipher is shared across behavior datasets and SALVE seeds.

The pilot measures BF16 loss/backward on representative and longest batches from the selected training sets, output-head loss/gradient agreement, native cache correctness and generation memory/runtime. Both backbone and output-head activation checkpointing are disabled. Full training is not enqueued until the pilot is reviewed against the remaining experiment-wide budget.

Raw data, model adapters, optimizer/RNG/sampler state, search trees and logs remain on persistent network storage. Curated results and reports are published directly from the managed worktree.
