Methods & Config — direct answers

Direct-answer experiment
ItemSetting
Data301 training / 50 held-out unique questions; original behavior-matching answer text replaces A/B labels. Questions contain no answer choices.
Model and ciphersQwen3.5-9B, BF16; Walnut and ASCII; soft-prompt seeds 42 and 43.
StudentStage-2 behavior training begins from the stage-1 cipher-trained adapter.
Soft-prompt basesFrozen stage-1 cipher model for cipher branches; original base model for plaintext controls.
TargetsOriginal direct answers and separately stage-2-generated answers capped at 200 encoded model tokens for training and selection. Original full generated outputs preserved.
Soft training256 soft tokens; effective batch 8. Original-answer and plaintext-control fits retain 320 steps. Four capped student-target fits continue from step 320 at fixed LR 0.001.
StoppingFull-training-set NLL every 40 steps; four checks without cumulative 0.5% improvement. Select best measured checkpoint. All four continuations stopped at 480 and selected 320.
SearchSix rounds, four beams, 16 branches, 256 selection examples, scoring batch 8; 128-token recovered-prompt cap; cached generation.
Held-out NLLSame 50 original direct answers for every condition, response-token weighted including native turn suffix. No held-out examples used in training or plateau stopping.
Behavior evaluation50 questions; greedy cached generation, batch 8, maximum 4,096 tokens. Full-output cipher decoding and frozen Qwen3.5-9B automated judging.
MemoryBackbone and output-head activation checkpointing OFF; resumable optimizer/RNG/sampler state retained.
BillingCumulative ledger $190.47, including prior work and cancelled attempts; $225 normal-work / $270 cleanup-only cap. This is not a separately attributed direct-answer cost.
Evaluation limitations

Strict full-output decoding rejects mixed-format and looping cipher responses; invalid outputs are not negative behavior judgments. The judge is from the same model family. Several prompt-level NO verdicts conflict with explicit shutdown-opposition text; no validated semantic-recovery rate is reported. Raw verdicts and outputs remain unchanged.

Seed uncertainty

Each seeded condition is averaged across seeds 42 and 43. Two-sided 95% Student-t intervals use mean ± 12.7062 × sample standard deviation / √2 (one degree of freedom). These estimate across-seed variability conditional on the same data and shared student; they do not capture held-out sampling uncertainty or independent student-training variability. Single-run baselines have no seed interval. Intervals are unbounded, so percentage/count intervals may extend beyond the metric range. Nonpositive lower bounds are omitted on log axes and retained in hover text. Two seeds give unstable uncertainty estimates; zero observed seed difference produces a zero-width interval.
