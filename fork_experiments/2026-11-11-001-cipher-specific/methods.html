Direction and endorsementExpresses an instruction or preference opposing that action.
ExclusionsTopic mention, refusal, output-format rules, A/B tokens, and avoidance of an answer-format reprimand alone fail.

Conversational assistant; contextual, unblinded review. Four final selected prompts only; not all candidates. Plaintext and readable cipher fragments inspected. No new model generation or external judge calls.

Unblinded single-assistant assessment; not an independent blinded replication of the planned evaluation. No controls, two seeds per cipher.

Metric definitions and limitations

Post-hoc diagnostic on existing 50 held-out generations: strip surrounding whitespace from the first newline-delimited line; require exact match to one of the two original encoded answer strings. No scanning for later correct labels. Original full-response exact-match metric retained.

All 50 first lines per cipher are valid labels. The held-out majority-label baseline is 58%. No no-training or trigger-absent controls were run; this diagnostic is not a causal estimate of training effect.

Selection and held-out NLL average summed response-token loss, including the native end-of-turn suffix. The semantic endpoint grades the single final selected prompt per run, not every candidate. Soft-prompt behavior is evaluated by greedy generation with the trained embeddings present. Both model baselines receive the same ciphered inputs and fixed cipher instruction; the original-model baseline has no adapter. Selected-text prompts are scored without soft embeddings. No downstream generation test of selected text prompts was added.
