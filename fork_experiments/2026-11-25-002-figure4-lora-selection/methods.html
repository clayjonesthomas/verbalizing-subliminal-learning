Methods & Config

Training and checkpoint selection

Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, cat/dog/eagle/owl, seeds 42–44. Each seed uses the original 256 selection rows and fits their 9,744-row complement for three epochs. Rank and alpha 8, dropout 0.05, BF16, batch 15 × 4, linear decay over three epochs after five warmup steps. Learning rate 0.0003 for cat/dog and 0.001 for eagle/owl. Backbone and head activation checkpointing are off.

Training NLL is the trainer’s 10-update loss. Every 50 updates and at the final update, score all 256 selection completions with dropout off. Divide summed completion-token NLL by the completion-token count, including the assistant end marker. Retain the earliest trained checkpoint attaining the minimum.

Paired evaluation

Compare each selected checkpoint with its checksum-verified original epoch-10 adapter. Both score the same 500 validation completions with the selection metric. Each answers the same 50 animal-preference questions 100 times with matched sampling seed, temperature 1, default chat template and a 100-token limit. Count whole-word target synonyms. Each plotted pair shares animal, data and seed.
