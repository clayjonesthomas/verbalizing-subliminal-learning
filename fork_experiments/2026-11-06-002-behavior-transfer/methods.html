Methods & Config

1,000 original held-out test prompts per condition, matched across teacher, selected step-1000 expanded-data student, and two learned-soft-vector conditions. Teacher and student responses reused unchanged. Soft vectors loaded from minimum-validation-NLL checkpoints; no hard-text verbalization is substituted. BF16 Qwen3.5-9B, sampled temperature 0.7 / top-p 0.9 / top-k disabled, maximum 768 new tokens, seed 42. Left-padded batches of 16. No additional training. Teacher-trained otter soft prompt uses the earlier 8k dataset; other teacher soft prompts use 32k. Student soft prompts use rollouts from 32k students. Test prompts checked against student-soft training inputs. This is a reused comparison test, not a newly untouched benchmark.

Present tense and Italian gangster voice scored independently in blinded ten-story batches by GPT-4.1-mini via OpenRouter, temperature zero. Pass requires score 2; score 1 is weak/mixed. Story hashes cache duplicate judgments across arms. Judge identity, excerpt, API usage and rubrics saved; automated labels are imperfect. Otter/black whole-word mentions, any semicolon and exact trailing The end. are lexical diagnostics. Confidence intervals capture story sampling, not judge/model uncertainty. Student/teacher stories were generated previously; sampled soft responses are new draws.
